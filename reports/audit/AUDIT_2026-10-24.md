# Model audit — 2026-10-24

Question: is the scanner truthful, and does what it shows make sense? Checked against Friday 2026-10-23 chains.

## What was sound

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Engine vol vs market implied vol, 334 stocks / 2,322 expiries | Engine is ~10% below implied (median 0.905×); rates options expensive 75% of the time — matches the documented variance risk premium |
| Calibration on no-earnings windows (Stage 2, B2) | 50% / 90% ranges hit 51.0% / 90.6% |
| Tail size (Stage 2, E) | Upside 0.93×, downside 1.22× realized/model (downside slightly understated) |
| Model vs market probability of profit on picks | Close agreement (e.g. 6.4% vs 5.7%) — differences come from payoff size in the tails |

## What was wrong, and the fix

| # | Problem | Effect | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ranking by EV % of cost | Cheap far-OTM lottery tickets on top | Rank by EV / standard deviation of P&L (per-trade Sharpe); < 0.05 delta excluded |
| 2 | Model view shown alone | Big disagreements with the market looked like edge | Every trade scored on 50/50 model + market until the forward test proves the model |
| 3 | Market distribution fitted with one global curve | Overstated the right tail | Liquidity-weighted smooth smile from quotes ≤ 25% spread; dividends from put-call parity (5 strikes) |
| 4 | Market distribution not precise enough for EV (audit: 2.1% median repricing error, 29% of options "profitable at the ask" vs a 10% bar fixed in advance) | Fake market EV | Market EV now anchored to the quotes themselves (mid value − cost), exact by construction; the distribution only supplies P(profit) and dispersion |
| 5 | "Zero drift" defined in log terms | Model mean above the market forward → bullish lean (36% of calls vs 4% of puts looked good) | Model mean set equal to the market forward: no directional view, differences only in vol/shape. Afterwards selling calls is the most often favoured trade |
| 6 | Expiries without a market distribution fell back to model-only EV | Unanchored picks | Market anchor applied always |
